Duolingo set our Innovators Forum class a challenge. Our answer is Fold with Nara: a booth where an iPad app, with Nara the deer, takes you through folding an origami model. I led the team and created the software, the tech side and the presentation.

The brief

Duolingo came to our Innovators Forum class at the Iovine and Young Academy and set a challenge. Our team answered it with Fold with Nara. I led the team.

The booth

A wooden stand holds an iPad above a folding tray, with a projector clipped to the top. The iPad runs the app I built.

Nara, a cartoon deer, greets you on the start screen. When you finish a model, the app names it in Japanese, here 富士山 (Fujisan), shows your XP and the questions you answered, and asks whether you’ll take it home or hang it in the tent.

The demo

We showed it as a presentation with the booth: the projector, the interactive iPad and a working demo. I made the presentation.
