Low-cost prosthetic arm

My role
Research assistant, Stevens Institute of Technology, summer 2024, under faculty supervision; continued through Avenues’ Mastery Program.
Year
2024
Type
Prosthetic arm, research
Status
Working build
  • 90+ prototypes, from PLA finger test prints to a full printed hand and forearm.
  • Servos in the forearm pull Kevlar cords to close each finger; elastic cords open it again.
  • Sized to the wearer: the parametric model is built on seven hand measurements.

A 3D-printable lower-arm prosthetic, hand and forearm, built from off-the-shelf parts, driven by servos through tendon-style cords, controlled by a myoelectric sensor, and sized to the wearer from hand measurements.

    White palm and glossy black forearm; hand, wrist collar and end plate print as separate segments.

    5 forearm subsystems · servos, wrist control, Arduino, batteries, myoelectric control

How it works

One elastic cord holds the finger open; a Kevlar cord from a servo in the forearm pulls it closed. Elastic runs outside the bend, Kevlar inside.
