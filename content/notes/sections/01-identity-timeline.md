### 1.6 Identity summary (one paragraph, facts only)

Alexander Tully — Alex — is a high-school hardware builder finishing Avenues: The World School's
Mastery Learning Program with the class of 2026 (content/source/resume/resume-current.png), and
starting at USC's Iovine and Young Academy in Arts, Technology and the Business of Innovation in
fall 2026, alongside the TroyLabs BUILD accelerator's PM division (CLAUDE.md). He founded Tully
Tech, whose inventions on his résumé are a prosthetic arm buildable for under $100, the AntiCam
personal privacy wearable, and a solar road that charges electric cars in motion
(content/source/resume/resume-current.png). He has been a research assistant on prosthetics at
Stevens Institute of Technology (summer 2024), a selective intern at Octura, a Bank of America
partner (summer 2024), an NYU Tandon cybersecurity program student who won its hackathon (2023),
and a Take the World Forward fellow with Harvard College, MIT Solv[ED], and Learn with Leaders
(2021–2022) (content/source/resume/resume-current.png). In FIRST Tech Challenge robotics he is
captain and operations lead, and led electrical design, documentation, and mentoring for a team
that was twice NYC Champion on the Inspire Award and placed second for the Think Award at the
World Championship (content/source/resume/resume-current.png;
content/source/portfolio/03-honu-robot.jpg#page-1). He co-founded a program that trained 70+
students in CAD, printing, prototyping, fabrication, and shop safety
(content/source/resume/resume-current.png). He describes himself as an adaptable problem solver
with design, coding, and leadership experience, and as passionate about product design,
engineering, and entrepreneurship (content/source/resume/resume-current.png). He can be reached at
tullytech.com and alexptully@gmail.com (content/source/resume/resume-current.png).
