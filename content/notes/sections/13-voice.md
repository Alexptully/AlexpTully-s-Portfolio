### Themes

- User control over when one is seen, not concealment
- Harmless by physics — light, not radio; physics, not patterns
- A zone, not a kill switch — removes the person from the frame, not the camera from service
- Built to protect people, not to hide crimes
- Honest about limits — states weaknesses unprompted (e.g. weaker in daylight)
- Firsts and superlatives, hedged with a survey ("out of ~4000 teams surveyed")
- Quantify everything — iteration counts, percentages, milliseconds, before/after numbers
- Modularity and repairability as selling points in their own right
- Iteration framed as V1/V2/V3, each with a measured improvement
- Regenerative, networked leadership — hydra metaphor (cut off one head, two grow back)
- Growth metaphor for team development — seeds → roots → blossom
- Conversion funnel — exposure → participation → leadership
- Outreach as scale plus touch — high-scale curriculum, high-touch mentoring
- Gracious Professionalism and Coopertition (FIRST values)
- Documentation and measured impact as their own virtue
- Cost stated as a design constraint — always a ceiling or a comparison
- Leads with the person affected, then works back to the mechanism
- Failure treated as informational feedback, not a setback
- Biomimicry as a named design method
- Pairs a technical outcome with an ethics question — does it work, should it exist
- Reaches out to experts unprompted, as method rather than luck
- Teaching and building treated as inseparable
- Constraints framed as help, not restriction
- Prefers "accessible" and "affordable" over "disruptive" or "revolutionary"
- Self-description as maker and entrepreneur together, not student or engineer
- Defines a product by what it refuses to do ("Unlike camera jammers...")
- Dry, self-deprecating, sports-adjacent humor as a single aside
- Plain, unhyped register — concrete numbers, no adjectives of praise
- Problem stated in dollars and population counts first, solution as a requirements checklist second
- Paper blueprint as a physical bridge between design and material
- Machine parameters reformulated as editable design elements
- Assembled object, not print sequence, as the primary representation
