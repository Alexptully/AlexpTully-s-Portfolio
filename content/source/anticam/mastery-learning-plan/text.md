MASTERY LEARNING SYSTEM
Student / Team Alex Tully
Background
Provide context for how
you arrived at this work
Over the past two years, I have been working to create a company,
Tully Tech, focused on a product called AntiCam. AntiCam is a
personal privacy device that uses infrared light and reflective
materials to block hidden cameras and paparazzi, preventing
unwanted photography. I have, over the years, created multiple
prototypes, but they have all been unusable for a consumers, either
lacking certain functionalities or having impractical designs.
Goal
Describe the goal you
are trying to achieve
The goal of this project is to create a device that solves some of the
fundamental issues that have appeared in anticam over the years.
These major issues include not being able to block paparazzi
cameras, not working in certain lighting conditions, a usable form
factor, and ease of daily use. By solving these issues, I will have
created a fully functional, usable device that can be manufactured and
sold.
Final Deliverable(s)
Describe any final
deliverables & explain
how they satisfy the goal
The final deliverable will be a fully functional baseball cap version of
Anticam, capable of blocking most cameras and achieving Anticam's
privacy goals. This version of anticam should incorporate: a
light-detection system for flashes (to trigger the infrared lights),
proper lighting conditions for activation, retroreflective materials built
into the hat lining, a safe recharging system, and a comfortable
design. Included with the anticam device should be a accompanying
circuit diagram of how it could be easily recreated and manufactured.

Waves
1. Describe common types of waves and their characteristics
including amplitude, frequency, wavelength, propagation,
and transformation
a. The AntiCam device demonstrates wave principles by
utilizing infrared light, a type of electromagnetic wave
with a specific wavelength longer than visible light. The
device increases the light's amplitude by using strong
infrared LEDs to project intense energy, which
successfully overwhelms the camera sensor to obscure
the image. Light propagates from the device toward the
camera, and the design further leverages wave
transformation through reflection by incorporating
retroreflective materials that bounce any camera flash
directly back to its source, fulfilling the requirement to
describe these wave characteristics.
2. Describe wave phenomena including reflection, refraction,
diffraction, interference, polarization, and the Doppler
effect
a. AntiCam leverages the phenomenon of reflection by
using specialized retroreflective surfaces that return
incoming light waves, such as a flash, back to their
origin to prevent clear photography. As electromagnetic
energy, the infrared output undergoes refraction when
entering a camera lens and exhibits diffraction while
passing through the aperture. The project further
addresses wave interference and polarization by
examining how overlapping visible and infrared signals
impact sensors, or through the strategic integration of
filters to refine the device's privacy capabilities.
