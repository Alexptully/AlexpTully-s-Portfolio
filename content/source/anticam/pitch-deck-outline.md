# AntiCam — Pitch Deck Outline (TroyLabs BUILD application + investor-ready)

**Storyline (one paragraph).** Cameras are everywhere — in rentals, on doorbells, in every pocket — and once an image exists, AI can do anything with it. Nothing legal actually stops a camera from seeing you: jammers are federal crimes, detectors only find lenses after the fact, and anti-surveillance eyewear covers part of a face until the algorithms adapt. AntiCam lives in the gap between what cameras see and what people see: an infrared LED array washes out any night-vision-capable sensor, a retro-reflective lining bounces flashes back to the lens, and it ships as a cap, a clip-on pin, or a room unit for under $100 — harmless by physics, so nothing is jammed or damaged. It's real: two years, 30+ prototypes, four major versions, tested against a live doorbell camera. The first buyers are the ~35M Americans who worry about hidden cameras where they stay; parents, creators, journalists follow. Alex Tully is a hardware founder who ships (prosthetics research, published fabrication software, championship robotics) — and TroyLabs BUILD supplies exactly the design, product, finance and marketing teammates the next eight weeks need.
