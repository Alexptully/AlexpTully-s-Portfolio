Prototype
  • 30+ prototypes and four major versions, from laser-cut discs to clear acrylic pins.1

  • It works with light, not radio: unlike a jammer, which is illegal, it jams and damages nothing.6

AntiCam is my wearable privacy device: an infrared LED array and a retro-reflective lining that keep cameras from capturing a usable image of me, without jamming or damaging anything.

Versions

  1. First

    Proof of physics: a single IR module on laser-cut discs with four coin cells. Cameras see it, eyes do not.

  2. Second

    First enclosure: laser-cut MDF housing, logo engraved into the lid, carryable.

  3. Third

    Clip-on device: acrylic plates, nine hand-soldered coin cells joined with copper tape, tested against real cameras.

  4. Final V1

    7 June 2023

    The generation that passed the doorbell test: the finished pin, and the under-brim cap array beside it.

    Doorbell test passed2 · Ring doorbell, night mode, 7 June 2023

  5. Final V2

    Six clear acrylic plates, coin cells and a slide switch, in three colourways.

  6. Cap V2

    The final version: the emitter array moves under the brim of a cap, aimed where cameras look, with retro-reflective lining behind it.7
