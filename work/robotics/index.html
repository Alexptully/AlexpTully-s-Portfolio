Robots

  1. Scorpion

    Ulterior Motive #17253, 2022–24 · Fabrication lead

    A motor at the base of the scissor lift pushes the base beams together, and the geometry drives the structure vertically.

    Scorpion, #17253 · Fabrication lead

    • Design Award

  2. Ball-Drive

    Ulterior Motive #17253, 2022–24 · Operations lead and captain

    Four two-axis balls replace the wheels for more efficient, accurate positioning; it is designed on a master sketch so the parts stay linked.

  3. Swerve Drive

    #14712, 2024–25 · Electrical lead

    A working FTC-scale swerve drive, common in FRC and almost unseen in FTC, co-developed with another team; it competed at NYC-FIRST’s Robot-in-2-Days.

  4. Honu

    #14712, 2024–25 · Electrical lead

    Belt-driven wheels for customisable wheel torque and a 3-axis intake for quick sample transfer.
